From a full fridge to dinner — in four steps.

No more staring into the fridge. No more "what do we have?" Chefcam handles it.

01

Confirm your ingredients

Chefcam scans your fridge and lists every ingredient it detected. Tap to remove anything that's wrong, or add what we missed — you stay in control of the list.

02

AI matches the right recipes

Our model cross-references your exact ingredients against thousands of tested recipes from top food sites and ranks them by how well they fit what you have.

03

Browse what's possible tonight

See recipes sorted by match. A 100% match means you can cook it right now — no grocery run required. Filter by dish, prep time, or rating.

04

Open it and cook

Tap any recipe for full step-by-step instructions, ingredient quantities, a health score, and a link back to the original source — so you always know who tested it.
