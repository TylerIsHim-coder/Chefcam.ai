Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about scanning your fridge, finding recipes, and how Chefcam.ai fits into your kitchen routine.

How does Chefcam scan my fridge?

You take a photo inside your fridge (or pantry). Chefcam uses on-device vision to recognize ingredients and build an editable list. You can tap to fix mistakes or add items we missed — you stay in control.

Where do the recipes come from?

We match what you have to real recipes from trusted food sites and publishers — not generic AI-generated instructions. Each result links back to the original source so you know who developed and tested the dish.

Is Chefcam.ai free?

You can download the app and explore core features on the App Store. Any subscription or in-app purchase options are shown clearly inside the app before you commit.

Does Chefcam work without internet?

Some steps may require a connection to fetch recipes and sync your lists. Exact behavior can vary by version — check in-app settings or release notes for the latest.

What happens to my photos and data?

We’re serious about privacy. How we collect, use, and store data is described in our Privacy Policy (linked in the site footer). If you have questions about your account or data, contact us through the channels listed there.

Can Chefcam handle dietary restrictions?

The app helps you discover recipes that fit what’s in your fridge. Filters and preferences available in the app can help you narrow results — availability depends on the version you’re running.
