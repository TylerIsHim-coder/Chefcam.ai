Features

Chefcam connects what’s in your fridge to real recipes, nutrition context, and quick meal logging — so planning dinner doesn’t start from zero.

Fridge scanning

Snap the inside of your fridge and Chefcam identifies ingredients automatically. Edit the list anytime — add what we missed or remove what isn’t yours — so suggestions always match reality.

Recipe generation

We cross-reference your ingredients with tested recipes from trusted food sites — not vague AI-only instructions. See what you can cook tonight, ranked by how well it fits what you already have.

Calorie & macro tracking

Set calorie and macro goals and see how recipes and meals stack up. Track progress over time so “what should I eat?” stays aligned with how you want to eat.

Meal scanning

Log meals by scanning what’s on your plate — Chefcam helps capture what you ate so your day’s totals stay accurate without tedious manual entry.
